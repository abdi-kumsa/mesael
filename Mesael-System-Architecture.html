Why it is drawn this way

The layers matter as much as the boxes. Everyone reaches the system through a controlled way in, never directly into the data. Every request passes through one core that checks identity, budget, approval authority and documents before anything moves. The twelve business domains own their own rules but share one set of records. And the outside world — the bank, the tax portal, Peachtree — connects at the edge, so it can change without disturbing anything inside.

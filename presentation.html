Architecture

Role-Based Dashboards

Tailored interfaces ensuring each stakeholder only sees what they need.

  • Leta (Project Engineer): Requests payments, uploads documents, and tracks budget limits.
  • Dembi (Operations Manager): Technical verification and first-level approvals.
  • Mesael (General Manager): Executive oversight and final financial authorization.
  • Kalkidan (Treasury): Payment execution, cheque management, and ledger reconciliation.
  • System Admin: Governance, authority rules, and delegation management.

Compliance

The Four-Document Gate

Enforcing strict documentation requirements before any payment is authorized.

  • Mandatory Verification: The system physically blocks voucher submission unless the core documents are attached (Proforma, GRN, Store Issue, Supplier Invoice).
  • Real File Attachments: Direct PDF/image uploads integrated directly into the workflow.
  • Document Versioning: If a proforma is updated, the system tracks the new version (e.g., v2) while keeping old ones safely in the audit history.

Governance

Dynamic Approval Matrix

Smart routing based on real-time financial data.

  • Configurable Thresholds: Approval rules are not hardcoded. The admin defines limits (e.g., Dembi can approve up to 500,000 ETB).
  • Automated Routing: Vouchers automatically escalate to the correct authority (e.g., Mesael) based on the financial value.
  • Budget Validation: Real-time checks against Project Cost Codes. Requests exceeding committed budgets are flagged instantly.

Continuity

Out-of-Office Delegation

Ensuring zero workflow bottlenecks when decision-makers are away.

  • Proxy Approvals: Managers can temporarily delegate their approval authority to another user.
  • Time-Bound Security: Delegations have strict start and end dates automatically enforced by the system.
  • Transparent Audit Trails: The immutable audit log explicitly records: "Approved by [Proxy] on behalf of [Manager]".

Treasury

Payment Execution

Bridging approvals to actual cash flow seamlessly.

  • Execution Queue: Once fully approved, vouchers land strictly on Kalkidan's desk.
  • Bank Balance Protection: The system verifies the selected bank account has sufficient funds before allowing a release.
  • Automated Ledger Deduction: Releasing a payment instantly deducts the system bank balance and permanently marks the voucher as PAID.

Security

Strict Cheque Control

Complete visibility and tracking over physical cheque assets.

  • Chequebook Registration: All physical chequebooks are registered into the system with their exact starting and ending leaf numbers.
  • Status Tracking: Every individual cheque leaf is tracked (BLANK, ISSUED, CLEARED, VOID).
  • Automated Burning: When Kalkidan executes a payment via cheque, she selects a specific blank leaf. The system binds that exact cheque to the voucher.

Integration

Statutory Sync & ERP

The Peachtree Guarantee for legal compliance.

  • Staged Exports: The platform acts as the operational brain, while Peachtree remains the legal statutory ledger.
  • Controlled Sync: Clean, matched journal entries are staged and exported in bulk, avoiding messy retroactive backlogs.
  • Tax Ready: Built-in modules for managing VAT declarations, Withholding Tax (WHT), and ERCA compliance certificates.

Scale

Expanded Capabilities

Beyond core finance into full operational management.

  • Subcontractor IPCs: Managing interim payment certificates, advance deductions, and retentions.
  • Payroll & Statutory: Tracking timesheets, generating payslips, and managing pension/income tax liabilities.
  • Equipment & Cash: Daily cash tracking and machinery deployment costs handled natively.
